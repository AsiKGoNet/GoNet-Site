Data deletion

Last updated: November 9, 2026

How to ask for your data to be deleted from GoNet's systems.

Contacts of an organization

  1. Email asi@go-net.co.il with the subject "Data deletion request".
  2. Include your name, phone number and the name of the organization that messaged you.
  3. We will confirm the request, coordinate it with the organization, and delete the data within 30 days, except data the law requires us to keep (such as receipts).
To just stop receiving WhatsApp messages, reply STOP (or "הסר") to any message.

Organizations that connected their WhatsApp account

  1. In the system: ניהול (Management) → חיבור WhatsApp (Connect WhatsApp) → disconnect. The access token Meta gave us is no longer used.
  2. In Meta: remove our app's access in your business settings at business.facebook.com.
  3. To also delete what we stored (the token, the account IDs and the message history), email asi@go-net.co.il.
