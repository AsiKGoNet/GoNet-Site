Accessibility statement

Last updated: November 11, 2026

גונט פתרונות מחשוב בע"מ (GoNet) builds and runs the website go-net.co.il. We believe in giving everyone equal service, and in improving the service we give to people with disabilities.

Accessibility level

The site was adapted to the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013, and to Israeli Standard 5568 at level AA, which is based on the W3C WCAG guidelines. The accessibility work was done by the company itself. The site supports common browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) and mobile phones.

Using the accessibility menu

An accessibility icon sits in the corner of every page. Clicking it opens the accessibility menu. Each option takes effect immediately; click it again to turn it off, or reset all settings. Your choices are saved in your browser and apply on every page. The menu also works from the keyboard: Tab to move, Enter to select and Esc to close.

What we did on the site

  • Semantic page structure (H1/H2/H3 headings, navigation and content regions) for assistive technologies such as NVDA and JAWS.
  • Simple, clear navigation and clearly organized content.
  • Everything works from the keyboard, with a clearly visible focus indicator.
  • A "Skip to content" link at the top of every page.
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and background.
  • Alternative text (alt) for every image.
  • Responsive layout for all screen sizes, with browser zoom up to 200% without losing content.
  • Each page declares its language (Hebrew or English) so screen readers read it correctly.

Accessibility menu options

  • Larger and smaller text.
  • High contrast.
  • Highlighted links.
  • Readable font with wider letter, word and line spacing.
  • Stop animations and motion.
  • Reset settings.

Exceptions

Despite our efforts, some parts of the site may not yet be fully accessible. We keep improving the site's accessibility as part of our commitment to let everyone use it, including people with disabilities.

Contact about accessibility

If you run into an accessibility problem, please contact our accessibility coordinator. It helps if you include:

  • A description of the problem.
  • What you were trying to do.
  • A link to the page.
  • Your browser and its version.
  • Your operating system.
  • The assistive technology you used, if any.
