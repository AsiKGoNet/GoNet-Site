GoNet is a leading technology company in consulting, management and development of custom projects and standard products, deployed in large organizations across the Israeli economy.

GoNet works with today's most advanced technologies in the high-tech industry. The company focuses on complete computing solutions built on advanced Microsoft environments, with Oracle as the database, and leads in delivering a wide range of services and software development.

GoNet puts its customers first. Its core goals are a culture of service, reliability, professionalism, fast development and high standards.
