Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what personal information גונט פתרונות מחשוב בע"מ, company no. 512968744 ("GoNet", "we"), collects, how we use it, and how to request access to it or deletion of it. It covers the website go-net.co.il and the systems we develop and run for organizations, including WhatsApp messaging.

1. Who is responsible for the data

In the systems we run for organizations, the organization owns the data about its contacts, and we process it on the organization's behalf and according to its instructions. If you are a contact of such an organization, you can also contact the organization directly. For this website and for inquiries sent to us, GoNet is responsible for the data.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details: name, phone number, email and address.
  • Data the organization manages in the system: registrations, attendance, payments and receipts, certificates and notes.
  • WhatsApp messages: the phone number, the content of the messages sent, their delivery and read status, and the replies received from contacts.
  • Messaging consent: whether the contact agreed to receive messages, and opt-out requests.
  • System users: the login details of the organization's staff and the actions they performed.

Payment card details are processed by licensed payment providers, according to their requirements.

3. WhatsApp messages

Messages are sent through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. To send a message, the phone number and the message content are passed to Meta, which processes them under the WhatsApp Business Policy and the WhatsApp Privacy Policy. Messages are sent only to contacts who agreed to receive messages from the organization.

Stop receiving messages: reply STOP (or "הסר") to any message and the organization will not send you further messages. You can also contact the organization or us.

4. Connecting an organization's WhatsApp account

An organization can connect its WhatsApp Business account to the system with Facebook Login for Business. When it does, Meta gives us an access token and the IDs of the business portfolio, the WhatsApp Business account and the phone number. We use them only to send and receive messages on the organization's behalf and to manage its message templates. We never receive the Facebook password. The organization can revoke our access at any time, from the system or from its Meta business settings.

5. How we use the data

  • Running the system for the organization: registrations, payments, attendance and reports.
  • Sending the messages the organization asked to send: confirmations, reminders and updates.
  • Showing contacts' replies to the organization's staff.
  • Support, security and fixing problems.
  • Meeting legal requirements.

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

6. Who we share data with

  • Meta / WhatsApp: to send and receive WhatsApp messages.
  • Service providers: hosting, email and payment processing, only as needed to provide the service.
  • Authorities: when the law requires it.

7. Retention and security

Data is kept while the organization uses the service, or as long as the law requires. We use standard security measures: encrypted connections, role-based permissions, and encryption of the Meta access tokens.

8. Your rights

Under the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, you may access the data about you and ask to correct or delete it. How to request deletion is explained on the data deletion page.
